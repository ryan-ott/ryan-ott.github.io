Howdy!

I'm Ryan, a MSc student of Artificial Intelligence at the University of Amsterdam, currently in my last semester.

Throughout my studies, I've developed a strong passion in particular for `Model Interpretability`, finding ways to make complex models more understandable and transparent. I'm also interested in `Computer Vision`, `Natural Language Processing`, and the intersection of the two in `Multimodal Learning`.
Currently I'm working on my master thesis, which is about integrating `Explainable AI` in Car-Drivetrain Quality Assurance models at BMW Group in Munich.

If you'd like to talk about anything not related to AI, just start a conversation about literally any sport imagineable and we'll be good to go, or just tell me about your favorite movie! If not, you can probably find me at the nearest karting track 🏎️
